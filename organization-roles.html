Annexure E: Financial Logistics & Cash Flow Mechanics

To ensure complete transparency, the following rules dictate exactly how cash flows between Customers, Riders, Partners, and the Company, exactly as programmed in our backend Settlement Module.

  • Rider Cash Collection (COD Protocol): Every Rider has a digital wallet built into the Rider App. When a customer pays ₹500 in physical cash for an order, the rider keeps the cash in hand, but their digital wallet balance immediately drops by ₹500. If their wallet balance drops below the allowed negative limit (e.g., -₹2000), our backend system will automatically block their account, meaning they cannot receive any new orders. To unblock the account, the rider must click "Deposit Cash" in their app and pay the pending amount back to BazarChowk using our integrated Razorpay UPI portal.
  • Restaurant/Partner Payouts: Every time a restaurant or salon completes an order, it is recorded safely in our Prisma Database. Every night at 12:00 AM, our backend cron job automatically runs the Settlement Module. The system calculates the total sales for the day, deducts the BazarChowk Platform Commission (based on the specific Partner Type), and deducts the applicable GST. The remaining final payout amount is then pushed to the Razorpay Route API, which automatically deposits the money into the Partner's registered bank account on a T+1 (next day) basis.
  • Rider Earnings Calculation: Riders do not keep the COD cash as their salary. Instead, they earn a Base Delivery Fee plus a Distance Fee, which is calculated accurately using live map tracking data in the backend. These earnings are safely stored in their "Earnings Wallet" and are transferred automatically to their personal bank accounts every week.
